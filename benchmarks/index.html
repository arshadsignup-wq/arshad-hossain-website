How these figures are produced

  • Read directly from Meta Ads Manager, Google Search Console or Google Analytics on accounts I managed.
  • Nothing is modelled, estimated or extrapolated. Where a campaign is still running and a final figure would mislead, the table says so rather than dividing two numbers.
  • Each figure links to the case study carrying the screenshot it came from. Two clients are anonymous at their request; that is noted on their pages.
  • Markets are never blended. US lead-generation costs and Bangladeshi conversational-commerce costs live on separate pages because averaging them would produce a number that is true of nothing.

If you find a figure on this site you can't trace to a screenshot, tell me and I'll either produce it or remove the claim.
