Wardrobe By Syra, Conversion Campaigns

Facebook Conversion Ads · Bangladesh

Objective

Drive direct website sales at the lowest possible cost per purchase using product-specific offers.

Strategy

  • Ran 2 separate conversion campaigns optimized for website purchases: a Tote Bag low-budget test and a T-shirt volume campaign
  • Focused on clear product visuals, limited-time offers, and direct "Shop Now" CTAs
  • Targeted high-intent buyers using retargeting and lookalike strategies
  • Used dynamic creatives to test image/copy variations
  • Actively monitored cost-per-purchase and scaled only when cost stayed under $1

Results

121

Total website purchases

$0.71

Avg cost per purchase

28

Tote Bag purchases at $0.32 each, $9.01 total spend

93

T-shirt purchases at $0.82 each, $76.68 total spend
