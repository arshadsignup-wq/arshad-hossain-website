Objective

Overcome the lowest sales season (August-September) and boost outlet sales through a creative campaign that stood out from the generic discount ads flooding the market.

Strategy

  • Identified the problem: outlet sales dropped sharply in August-September despite stable online performance
  • Competitors were all pushing similar 25% discount ads, leading to ad fatigue
  • Instead of another plain offer post, built an entertainment-first reel
  • Downloaded a trending video from Zack D Films
  • Added a Bangla voiceover using Eleven Labs to localize the content
  • Inserted the 25% discount offer in the final 5 seconds with urgency (valid till September 1st)

Execution

  • Published the reel on Wardrobe By Syra's official page
  • The video blended humor, surprise, and urgency, making it highly shareable
  • Discount CTA was short and deadline-driven, prompting immediate outlet visits
  • Monitored real-time performance and tracked sales impact over the 2-day period (31st Aug - 1st Sept)
