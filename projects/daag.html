Daag · দাগ

Civic Accountability Archive

What it is

Built with my brother. Daag is a crowdsourced platform for documenting misogynistic online commentary in Bangladesh. The idea came from seeing harmful posts go unchecked on social media. The platform lets users submit evidence (screenshots, links, usernames), which goes through a moderation system before being published to a searchable archive. It includes profile search, analytics dashboards, and a full moderation console for reviewing submissions. The name "Daag" means "stain" in Bangla, because these words leave a mark.

Features

  • Evidence submission with screenshot uploads, links, and usernames
  • Moderation queue with approval and rejection workflows
  • Searchable archive of documented incidents
  • Profile search to look up repeat offenders
  • Analytics dashboard for tracking submission trends
  • Full moderation console for admin reviewers

Tech stack

HTML/CSS/JS · Supabase-ready · Deployed on Netlify
