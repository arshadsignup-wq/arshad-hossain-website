Is My Baby Alright?

Parenting Companion App

Why I built this

After becoming a father, I couldn't find a single app that actually helped with the 2am panic moments. Is the baby's temperature too high? Is this rash normal? Should I go to the ER? I built this app to answer those questions.

What it does

It includes developmental milestone tracking by age, a symptom triage wizard that helps you decide whether to wait or call a doctor, sleep and feeding logs, growth charts, vaccine schedules, and even a mental health screener for new parents (EPDS). It also has a caregiver handoff feature so you can share care instructions when someone else watches your baby.

Features

  • Developmental milestone tracking organized by age
  • Symptom triage wizard for quick decision-making
  • Sleep and feeding logs
  • Growth charts to track weight, height, and head circumference
  • Vaccine schedule tracker
  • EPDS mental health screener for new parents
  • Caregiver handoff notes for babysitters and family
