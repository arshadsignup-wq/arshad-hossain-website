QuillBill

Invoice & Proposal Generator

Why I built this

I needed a good invoicing tool but everything I tried was either too bloated, too expensive, or required creating an account. So I built QuillBill.

What it does

It has 15+ professional invoice templates, real-time preview as you type, PDF export, and shareable links. You can set up multi-currency billing and the whole thing works without any login or account. Just open, create, and send.

Features

  • 15+ professional invoice templates
  • Real-time preview as you type
  • PDF export with one click
  • Shareable invoice links
  • Multi-currency billing support
  • No account or login required

Tech stack

React · TypeScript · Vite
