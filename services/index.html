How engagements work

Two shapes, and I'll tell you which one fits after the audit.

  • Monthly retainer: ongoing campaign management or SEO. I run the account, you get the reporting. Month to month; no long lock-in, because if the numbers aren't moving you shouldn't be stuck.
  • One-off project: an audit, a tracking rebuild, a campaign launch, or a fixed-scope content plan. Priced per project.

Ad spend is separate and always paid by you, directly to Meta. It never passes through me, and I don't take a percentage of it. That model quietly rewards spending more rather than spending better.

What's included

  • Account audit before anything else, including tracking verification
  • Campaign structure, audience strategy and budget allocation
  • Creative direction and testing: I brief and iterate on creative, working with your assets or your designer
  • Pixel and Conversions API setup with event deduplication
  • Reporting you can actually read, including the months that go badly

What's not included

  • Ad spend
  • Design and video production: I direct creative, I don't produce it
  • Guaranteed rankings or guaranteed lead volume. Anyone offering those is selling you something else.

Proof, not promises

Every number on this site comes from an account I managed, and every case study shows the reporting behind it. Start with the case studies if you want the evidence before the sales pitch.

Common questions

What do you charge?

Ongoing management is a monthly retainer; audits and one-off work are project-priced. I quote after the free audit rather than before, because a price given without seeing the account is a guess. Ad spend is always separate and paid by you directly to Meta.

Is there a minimum ad spend?

Not a hard one, but below roughly $1,000 a month the maths gets difficult: a small budget can't generate enough conversion events for Meta's delivery system to learn, which is exactly the trap most struggling accounts are in. If your budget is under that, an audit or a one-off setup is usually better value than a retainer.

Do you work with agencies or only direct clients?

Both. I've run campaigns as a consultant inside other agencies' client work as well as directly for business owners.

How long before I see results?

Paid campaigns produce signal within days and meaningful optimisation within four to six weeks, because you're buying traffic immediately. SEO is slower: local map-pack movement can show in 30 to 90 days, competitive organic rankings usually take four to six months.

What if you can't help?

I'll say so at the audit stage. It costs me an hour and saves us both a bad engagement.

Not sure which of these you need?

That's what the audit is for. Send me the account or the URL and I'll tell you where the biggest gap is.
